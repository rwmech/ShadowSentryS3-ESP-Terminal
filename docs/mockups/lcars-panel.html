ShadowSentry S3: LCARS-style front panel, mockup v3 (Cerritos)

The board's 480 x 320 at 2x. Only the board's fonts and sizes: Montserrat 12, 14, 20 and 28. Every string was measured against the board's own LVGL font tables (C:\Users\rwmec\Documents\Development\Honeypot\mockups\fitcheck.py). The frame takes 52 px on the left and 20 px at top and bottom; content gets 400 x 282. The port table is the real list of ports the board listens on, highlighted in turn; a port hit today lights up. Hold is only for ACKNOWLEDGE; ignores and block ask to confirm. Your browser may not have Montserrat installed, so letter widths can differ slightly from the panel.

Pages

  • Rail: 01 to 05 pick a page and stop rotation at once. 06 AUTO (underlined while on). Selection: the selected box's bottom edge becomes a dark red line (the same red as the port scan) rotates the pages every 60 s; tap it to turn rotation on or off. No page dots.
  • 01 OPS: today's counts, events per hour with the scan line, the focus panel (rotates every 5 s: last event, Pi-hole, router, top trap), and the port table: the 35 ports the board listens on, zero padded, each in its page's rail colour (TRAPS, WIRES, NET), dim until hit today; the scan steps through them in dark red.
  • 02 TRAPS: the services that pretend to be something (Hikvision, camera stream, Telnet, SSH, FTP, ADB), hits today, last hit. Tap: who, what they sent.
  • 03 TRIPWIRES: services nothing should touch (RDP, VNC, SMB, Redis, MySQL, MSSQL, JetDirect, the MikroTik decoys) and the router tripwires. Bar colour: lit (hit today), armed, or off (router ones until syslog is on).
  • 04 NET and 05 DEV as before. DEV: tap a device, BLOCK asks to confirm. No log page on the panel: the log stays on the dashboard.
  • RED / YELLOW: the board's own emblem, smaller, no side bars, only its stripes move (1 s a stripe on red, 2 s on yellow). No frame flashing, no pulsing text.

Will the display keep up?

  • Scan line: a 2 x 48 px object stepping every 50 ms. LVGL redraws the old and new spot, about 200 px a frame, well under a millisecond of SPI at 60 MHz.
  • Port highlight: two 45 x 13 px cells every 150 ms.
  • Emblem stripes: two areas of about 146 x 18 px, already running on the board (YELLOW size), logged at under 5300 px a frame.
  • A page change redraws the 400 x 282 content box once (about 225 KB of pixels, roughly 30 ms of SPI). The SD card shares that bus; the page change is rare, the rest is small.
