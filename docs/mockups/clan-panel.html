What carries over from your references

  • Header: an emblem plate top left, a gold title on a red angled banner with a spaced subtitle, a SYSTEM STATUS plate with a NOMINAL pill (green, amber on caution, red on hostile), red glow strips under the banner.
  • Nav: an icon column of metal buttons, the selected one red with a glow: TACTICAL, BAIT, PERIMETER, SYSTEMS, CONTACTS, INFO.
  • Tactical page: a cyan holo radar with a live sweep, contacts as blips sorted the way the references do it: FRIENDLY (your admin PCs), NEUTRAL (known LAN devices), HOSTILE (sources that tripped something today). SHIELDS is Pi-hole's blocked share and POWER is free RAM, as segmented red and gold bars. An EVENTS / ALERTS plate with the last three.
  • The other pages: the same data as the LCARS pages in plates with red corner ticks.

On the board

  • The textures (scratched metal, rust) would be one 480 x 320 background image in flash, about 300 KB, plus a few small plate images; the rest is LVGL rectangles, lines and labels. Flash is at 2.3 MB of 6 MB.
  • The radar sweep is the one costly piece: a rotating wedge repaints a 150 x 150 area. On the board it would be a line or a narrow wedge drawn at about 15 frames a second, or stepped every 100 ms.
  • The 480 x 320 screen is a third of your references' size: fewer, larger panels per page, the 12 px minimum font kept.
